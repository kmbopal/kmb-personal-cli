KMB Personal CLI: privacy policy

KMB Personal CLI is a private tool that only Kevin Bartman uses, on his own computers. It reads, drafts and organizes his own Gmail and Google Calendar when he asks it to.

No one else uses it. What it reads stays with him and the tools he runs it with, and none of it is sold or shared.

To end its access, remove KMB Personal CLI at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
